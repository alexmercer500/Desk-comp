Your coding agents, watched by a tiny chai cup.

Kulhi is a desktop pet for Windows, and a companion for your AI coding agents. It tells you when Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and friends finish, and when they need you, with Allow and Deny right on its card.

06On your phoneAndroid, over your own Wi-Fi.

Leave the desk. Take the OK with you.

The Android app shows your agents, lets you Allow or Deny, and can reply to an agent that finished. It pairs with your PC over your own Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot.

Away from home, it can go through a relay you run yourself on Cloudflare's free plan, encrypted end to end. Settings on the PC walks you through making one.

08Set upAbout a minute.

Set up in a minute.

On your PC

  1. Download the installer Download for Windows. About 5 MB. It installs for you only, with no admin rights.
  2. Run it, even if Windows frowns Kulhi isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Connect your agents Settings → Coding agents. One switch each.

Your phone, if you like

  1. Get the Android app on your phone Open this page on the phone and tap Get the Android app. About 5 MB, for Android 11 or newer.
  2. Let it install Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it. If Play Protect says it doesn't know the app, choose More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Pair it with your PC On the PC: Settings → Phone → Link a phone → Show pairing code. Scan the code with the app. It updates itself after that.

Away from home

On the same Wi-Fi, the phone needs nothing more. To reach your PC from anywhere, you run a small relay on Cloudflare's free plan. It only passes messages along, encrypted: it can't read them.

  1. Make your relay With a free Cloudflare account and a GitHub account, open Cloudflare's deploy page, keep the name it suggests, and click Deploy.
  2. Copy its address Open the new Worker. Its address ends in .workers.dev.
  3. Paste it into Kulhi Settings → Phone → Away from this network → Relay address. Save, then turn on Reach your phone anywhere. Your phone picks it up by itself. Rather use a terminal? The full guide.

09QuestionsAnd answers.

Questions people ask.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now, and Linux is planned. On a Mac? Try coucou, the little friend in your Mac's notch that inspired Kulhi.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. The phone app is for Android only for now. Everything on the PC works without a phone.

Will it slow my PC down?

The 2D characters are light. Rui is in 3D; on a laptop he can hand over to a 2D friend while you're on battery.

How do updates work?

It checks GitHub every few hours. Updates are signed, and install only when you click.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, and its code is open source under the MIT licence. The optional Ask feature uses your own Anthropic API key.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it from Windows Settings. That takes out its hooks too, after backing up your agents' settings.
