# New character: the inventor and his big sister (proposal)

Status: **proposal, 16 October 2026.** Nothing is built yet. The decisions under
[Open questions](#open-questions) come first.

## The idea in one line

A small, serious boy inventor at his workbench, and his tall, sunny big sister
who keeps bursting in to "help". He does the work, she brings the chaos and the
love. It's a sibling *dynamic* we're borrowing (genius little brother vs.
cheerful, meddling big sister), not anyone's characters.

## What we keep, and what we must never copy

We keep the **relationship and the comedy beats**: he's focused and proud of
his machines; she's curious, can't resist touching things, presses the button he
told her not to press, and he's exasperated but secretly adores her.

We must **not** reproduce Dexter's Laboratory or its characters. Rules for every
drawing, prompt and line of code (like Dogesh's "Courage's style only" rule):

- **Names:** nothing like Dexter, Dee Dee, Mandark, or "Laboratory" in the name.
- **Him:** no orange or red hair, no white lab coat, no big square glasses, no
  purple gloves or boots, no fake accent.
- **Her:** no blonde pigtails, no pink ballet tutu or leotard, no ballet.
- **No catchphrases from the show** (not "What does this button do?", "Omelette
  du fromage", or "Get out of my laboratory!"), and no secret lab hidden behind a
  bookcase.
- **Show title cards, logos and theme music:** none, and nothing that imitates them.

A style can be shared (lots of cartoons use flat, geometric 90s shapes), but the
characters, names and gags are our own.

## Who they are

### Him: the little inventor (working name **Chintu**)

- **Personality:** eight-ish, very serious, very proud, talks like a professor,
  plans everything, sighs a lot. Loves his gadgets, secretly loves his sister.
- **Silhouette:** short and boxy. A big square-ish head, a compact rectangular
  body, short legs. He reads as a **square**.
- **Look:** black hair with one stubborn cowlick that springs up when he has an
  idea, warm brown skin, thick brows that do half his acting. Welding goggles
  pushed up on his forehead (they drop over his eyes when he's working
  seriously). A teal workshop apron over a mustard T-shirt, pockets full of
  pencils and a screwdriver. A chunky wrist gadget with a little screen.
- **His tells:** the cowlick (up = idea, flat = defeated), the goggles (down =
  concentrating), pushing up his sleeves before a big moment.

### Her: the big sister (working name **Didi**, "big sister" in Hindi)

- **Personality:** about thirteen, bubbly, fearless, endlessly curious, always
  dancing. She means well. She never reads instructions.
- **Silhouette:** tall and narrow, a **triangle**: a long flowing skirt, thin arms,
  a long braid down her back.
- **Look:** a **Kathak** dancer, our own twist instead of ballet. A marigold
  kurta top over a flared purple dance skirt, a long black braid with a small
  flower clip, ghungroo bells on her ankles that jingle when she moves, big
  happy eyes, a huge grin.
- **Her tells:** the braid (it whips round when she spins and droops when she's
  sorry), the ankle bells (you hear her before you see her, so the jingle is her
  entrance), Kathak spins (chakkars) whenever she's happy.

Together they read instantly at 160 px and even at the island's 50 px: **small
square next to a tall triangle**, teal and mustard next to marigold and purple.

## How they live in the app

He is the main character, always there. **She drops in**: she isn't on screen
all the time. That keeps the corner calm and makes her entrances funny, the way
she bursts into his room in the show's spirit. A setting decides how often (see
[Open questions](#open-questions)).

| Moment | Him | Her |
|---|---|---|
| Resting, nothing going on | Tinkers with a gadget, fiddles with his wrist screen | Now and then she spins across behind him, bells jingling, waves at you, spins off |
| You hover or poke | Pushes his goggles up, "Hmph?" | Poke her (when she's there) and she does a little spin |
| Working: editing | Goggles down, typing furiously at a small console | |
| Working: reading or searching | Blueprint scroll and a jeweller's loupe, cowlick twitching | |
| Working: running a command | Rolls up his sleeves, reaches for his big red button… | …and **she presses it first** ("Ooh!"). He facepalms. Their signature gag. |
| Working: on the web | A little periscope out of his backpack, scanning | |
| Waiting for your OK | Holds up a clipboard, taps it, looks at you | Runs to the front, jumping and waving at you: "He needs you!" |
| A question for you | Scratches his head, a "?" over him | Holds up two big cards with the options, like a game show |
| Task done | Triumphant fist pump, cowlick springs up | Bursts in, hugs him off his feet, confetti spin |
| One task done, others still running | A quick proud nod, back to work | |
| Something failed | A small puff of smoke, soot on his face, cowlick flat | Peeks in: "Oopsie… wasn't me!" and tiptoes out |
| You stopped the task | A long-suffering sigh | Pats his head |
| You're away / dozing | Falls asleep on his console, goggles askew | Tiptoes in, puts a blanket over him, tiptoes out |
| Picked up (dragged) | Dangles, arms crossed, offended | Grabs his ankle and comes along for the ride |
| Waking up for the day | Bursts out of a little workshop hatch, ready | |

Top-edge island: the head-and-shoulders view shows **him**. She pops in from
the side of the island for her moments (the button, the waiting wave, the
hug).

Outfits can come later (like Dogesh's): a science-fair suit, a raincoat, festival
clothes for both at Diwali.

## Art style: the same in 2D and 3D

**Mid-century, geometric cartoon style:**
- Flat colour, clean thick outlines, simple shapes (squares, triangles, circles).
- Little shading, a limited palette, very readable silhouettes.

It's the general late-90s TV-cartoon look, which no one owns. Our shapes,
colours and characters are our own.

**2D** (Canvas, drawn in code like the others): flat fills, a dark outline on
every shape, at most one soft shadow tone per colour, snappy poses with
squash and stretch.

**3D** (three.js): the same shapes built as simple solids (boxes, cylinders, a
cone skirt) with **toon shading and ink outlines**, the "anime" look Rui already
has (cel shader plus inverted-hull outlines), so the 3D version looks like the 2D
drawing turned into a figure rather than a different character. Same
proportions, same colours, same expressions in both.

So that the two stay identical, we make **one design sheet** first (front,
three-quarter, side, the expression set, the colour swatches) and build both
versions from it.

## How it would be built

This is the first character that is **two figures**, so it's the biggest one
yet. The app already has most of what it needs (states, working variants, cards
placed around the character's outline, the island, phone pictures).

1. **Design sheet first.** Concept art from the prompts below, or drawn in the
   2D preview page, for you to approve: both figures in four views, expressions,
   colours, and the "small square next to a tall triangle" silhouette at 160 px
   and 50 px. Nothing else starts until you're happy with it.
2. **2D version.** One new skin that draws both figures (his pose channels and
   hers side by side, so they crossfade like any skin) with every state and the
   five working variants above. Her drop-ins are scheduled inside the skin. Check
   it in the 2D preview page, then in the real app.
3. **3D version.** A new actor holding two small rigs, built from the existing
   rig helpers (springs, noodle limbs, toon look, outlines), with the same cues
   the stage already uses (`work`, `research`, `run`, `browse`, `call`,
   `ponder`, `hooray`…). Her entrances use the see-through room round the corner
   window, which already exists for Rui and Dogesh walking off-centre.
4. **Into the app.**
   - Character list, tray and Settings card (with the "how often she drops in" setting).
   - The clickable area covers both of them, so cards also avoid her.
   - Island framing on him.
   - Phone pictures for each mood, which needs a new Android build so the phone knows them too.
5. **Release** as a minor version (a new feature), and an Android version that
   knows the new character.

## Open questions

These are yours to decide before step 1:

1. **Names.** Chintu & Didi, or another pair from the table (or your own)?
2. **"Same art style in 2D and 3D".** My reading: one design that looks the same in
   both, in the flat, geometric 90s-cartoon style above. Is that what you meant?
3. **Her hobby.** Kathak dancer (my suggestion), or something else (roller
   skates, hula hoop, singing)?
4. **How often she drops in.** Proposed setting: *often / sometimes (default) /
   only for big moments* (the button, waiting for you, done).
5. **Both always on screen instead?** I recommend she drops in (calmer, funnier),
   but you may prefer seeing both all the time.
6. **Sounds.** Her ankle bells as her entrance sound (only when sounds are on)?
